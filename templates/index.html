FAQ + Field Notes

When do morels usually pop?

Usually in spring once soils warm into the sweet zone and recent moisture has the woods waking up.

Do indicator trees really matter?

They help narrow where you spend your time. Weather gets you in the ballpark, but tree choice and habitat can make the walk way more efficient.

What kind of places should I check first?

Hardwood edges, creek bottoms, old roots, leaf litter pockets, and soft terrain transitions that hold moisture longer.

Can people submit finds from anywhere?

Yes. The submission form asks for state and county so finds can stay useful without requiring a full exact location.
